# Comparison methodology

[Comparison home](README.md)

Research snapshot: **2026-10-14**. This is a growing, source-backed catalogue, not a claim to list every pedal ever manufactured. Hardware revisions and materially different models get separate records. Manufacturer product pages, manuals, effect lists and firmware notes take precedence over reseller specifications. Prices require seller/market sources and are not hardware specifications.

## Reading the data

- **? / unverified** = not established from the cited evidence. It does **not** mean no.
- **No** = an explicitly established absence or a complete, relevant manufacturer specification/manual that excludes the feature.
- **N/A** = the field does not apply (for example preset memory on a conventional analogue overdrive).
- **Partial** = the record has useful sourced facts but at least one requested field still needs verification. A model is not called fully verified merely because its product page was found.
- Manufacturer effect totals are retained as advertised, including `+` or `up to`. They are not rankings of sound quality and are not necessarily comparable between brands.

## Categories and size

Primary categories are guitar/multi-instrument processors, bass-focused processors, vocal/acoustic-vocal processors, and dedicated/specialist effects. A guitar processor with explicitly documented bass support can appear in a bass cross-reference without duplicating its underlying record. No implication that every guitar effect preserves a bass instrument's low end.

Within categories, separate **built-in expression pedal**, **external expression only**, and **neither/unverified**. An external-expression socket is not an included pedal. A MIDI controller is not itself an audio-effects processor.

Dimensions are in **millimetres**, normally manufacturer-labelled **width × depth × height**. Keep a note when a source uses length instead of depth, omits the pedal's maximum height, or conflicts with another source. Never substitute packaging dimensions. Weight is the unit's weight, not shipping weight; battery inclusion is noted when known.

The **200 × 140 mm** compact filter is `width <= 200 AND depth <= 140`, without rotating the unit. Additional bins: compact <=200×140; medium <=300×200 but not compact; large otherwise; unknown when either dimension is unverified. Footprint is not installed space: plugs, strain relief, power adapters and the expression-pedal sweep need additional clearance.

## Controls and live usability

Record rotary controls, footswitches and non-foot buttons separately. Push-encoders count once as rotary controls, not again as separate buttons. Concentric, independently adjustable shafts must be explicitly described rather than silently counted as one or two. Expression toe switches, power switches and small mode toggles are called out separately when verified. Touchscreen widgets are not physical buttons. Do not count palm-operated buttons as stage-safe footswitches.

Additional useful fields include switch modes, bypass/pass-through availability, preset-change gap or spillover (only when documented), tuner mute, looper duration/placement, drum patterns, parallel routing, dry blend, spillover and snapshot/scene support. No invented latency figures or subjective sound-quality scores.

## Effects and memory

Keep **maximum simultaneous processing blocks**, **available categories**, **choices per category**, and **overall advertised model count** separate. Fixed amp/cab/utility blocks are not necessarily freely assignable effect slots. DSP-limited products need that qualifier; two algorithms are not automatically two simple stompboxes. Amp, cabinet, IR and captured-model counts are separate from effects where the source permits.

For presets, distinguish total addressable slots, factory-filled slots, dedicated user slots, and overwriteable memory. `100 total / 55 factory-filled` is not `155 presets`. Never assume unused slots are the only editable slots. Library downloads are not additional onboard storage.

For synth/acoustic functions distinguish an electric-to-acoustic simulator, an acoustic amp model, acoustic-body IR loading, a true instrument synthesizer, a monophonic synth-like effect, vocal harmony, pitch correction and a vocoder. A required divided pickup or special cable must be disclosed.

## Connections, power and software

List connector sizes/types and counts where verified. Distinguish stereo pairs from a single TRS stereo jack and balanced mono TRS from stereo TRS. Headphone output gets its own field, including shared main-output arrangements. Do not equate a mic-input connector with phantom power. Record phantom voltage, instrument inputs for harmony detection, FX loops, MIDI DIN/TRS/USB/Bluetooth, USB-audio channel counts and S/PDIF where known.

USB connectivity does **not** imply USB-powered operation, audio-interface capability, editing, or MIDI support. Power notes distinguish voltage, polarity, specified current/draw versus adapter rating, battery type/capacity, rechargeability, and documented USB bus/PD powering. Battery runtime is manufacturer-rated, not an independently measured result.

Separate **full editor**, **preset/file manager**, **firmware updater**, **audio driver**, and **mobile audio compatibility**. Windows/macOS/iOS/Android support is only listed for the relevant documented application/function. Linux compatibility is not inferred from USB class compliance; where not established it remains unverified. Note optional Bluetooth adapters, paid apps/accounts and discontinued software where verified.

## IR, NAM and other captures

These are separate fields:

1. Third-party cabinet/body IR loading, slot count and sample limit when documented.
2. Native `.nam` loading and any documented architecture restrictions.
3. NAM conversion/optimisation into another format, such as Valeton SnapTone or explicitly documented NAM A2 workflows.
4. Proprietary captures/profiles, such as TONEX Tone Models, Neural Capture, Kemper Profiles or HeadRush/ReValver clones.

A capture/profile feature does **not** establish NAM compatibility. An IR loader is not an amp-capture loader. Factory cabinet simulations do not establish third-party IR support. USB updates alone do not establish any model-loading capability.

## Price policy

Used and new prices are separate columns. Each observed amount/range retains its **currency**, **market**, **condition**, **source**, and **check date**. USD is the common international comparison currency; Indonesian rupiah observations are shown separately where obtained. No silent currency conversion or claim that international pricing is an Indonesian delivered price.

A single seller's price is a single observation, not a market range. A used asking price is not a completed-sale value. Tax, shipping, import duties, accessories and condition may differ. Out-of-stock/discontinued listings and manufacturer list prices must be labelled. No fabricated secondhand prices, and no automatic percentage-of-new calculation presented as market evidence. Missing prices remain unverified.

## Coverage and maintenance

The comparison prioritises broadly available multi-effects, bass and vocal processors, then dedicated synth/acoustic/pitch/other effects. The coverage backlog explicitly records families still to research rather than disguising placeholders as completed comparisons. Check firmware-dependent facts and current prices before purchasing. Contributions should include primary specification links, exact model/revision, firmware context where relevant, and dated price evidence.
