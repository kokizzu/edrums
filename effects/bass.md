## Multi-instrument alternatives

Bass is not restricted to products labelled “bass” on the enclosure. The sourced [Valeton GP-100/150/180](guitar-compact.md), [BOSS GX-10/GT-1000/GT-1000CORE](guitar-boss.md), and other multi-instrument processors have documented bass-related processing. Compare input headroom, dry blend, DI output, parallel routing and low-end retention rather than assuming every guitar preset is appropriate for bass.
